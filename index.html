How I Work

Structured sourcing,
stage by stage

Three stages, one person accountable for all of them. You always know which stage your order is at.

01

Discovery

I analyse your product requirement, shortlist verified factories in the right category, and run background checks before any names reach you. You get catalogues, quotes and certifications to compare side by side.

+ Requirement and category analysis

+ Verified supplier shortlist

+ Background and capability checks

Typically 1 to 2 weeks

02

Order Finalisation

Once you've chosen a factory, I negotiate price, MOQ and payment terms, finalise the contract, and get pre production samples approved before a single unit goes into production.

+ Price and MOQ negotiation

+ Contract and payment terms

+ Pre production sample approval

Typically 2 to 3 weeks

03

Fulfilment

I stay on the factory floor through production, running inline, midline and final inspections, checking packaging and export documentation, and coordinating shipping so your order lands on time and as ordered.

+ Inline, midline and final inspection

+ Packaging and documentation checks

+ Shipping and logistics coordination

Through to dispatch

Every stage is documented as it happens. Inspection photos, factory quotes and production updates go straight to you, not filtered through a sales layer.
